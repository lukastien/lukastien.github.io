01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

The business problem

A robo-advisor's core job: given a client's risk profile, build a diversified portfolio that maximises risk-adjusted return — then prove, with risk numbers, how it compares to a plain benchmark.

This project implements that allocation engine with Modern Portfolio Theory: efficient-frontier optimisation across real historical prices, three risk tiers, downside-risk metrics, a rebalancing rule, and a benchmark comparison.

What's real vs synthetic (disclosed in the repo): price data is real — 5 years of daily data (2013–2018) for 30 securities across 8 sectors. Client risk profiles are synthetic (200 generated profiles, fixed seed) because no real client data exists for this project.

Optimisation

src/portfolio_optimization.py builds Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive tiers on the efficient frontier with PyPortfolioOpt (risk-free rate 2%). The Conservative 8% volatility target proved infeasible — the frontier floor was ≈9.71% — so it was built at that feasible floor and the adjustment is documented.

Risk measurement

Annualised Sharpe ratio, historical 95% Value-at-Risk, and maximum drawdown per tier, computed from the same return series so tiers and benchmark are directly comparable.

Rebalancing

A 5% drift rule: rebalance whenever any weight drifts more than 5% from target. In-sample trigger counts — Conservative 1, Moderate 5, Aggressive 6 — show how maintenance cost scales with volatility.

Reporting

Tableau-ready CSV exports (tier performance time series, risk/return summary, rebalance events) with a build guide, plus strategy and market-research briefs.

04 — CHALLENGES & DECISIONS

Challenges & decisions

An infeasible risk target

Challenge: The Conservative tier's 8% volatility target sits below the efficient frontier's minimum for this security set.

Decision: Build at the feasible floor (≈9.71%) and disclose it, rather than quietly relaxing constraints until the number appears.

No real bond data for the benchmark

Challenge: A true 60/40 needs a bond series the dataset does not contain.

Decision: Use defensive equities as an explicitly-labelled proxy and repeat that disclosure wherever the benchmark appears — never present it as a real bond allocation.

Concentration in the aggressive tier

Challenge: Long-only optimisation at a 22% vol target puts ~58% in a single name (AMZN).

Decision: Report the top weight alongside returns so the risk is visible; position limits are the natural next constraint to test.
